The Xiringuito

We belong to summer. The rest is beach

Set up on Ses Oliveres beach for several seasons, we are a wood-and-wicker terrace built around a tree that was here long before us.

Every season we come back and put the terrace up around the same tree. When summer ends we take it all down, and the beach is a beach again until next year.

FAQ

Before you come

Any other question? Give us a call.

872 20 40 41
Do I need to book?

Yes, during the season booking is essential. Call us on +34 872 20 40 41 and we'll keep a table for you. In winter the phone is on holiday too: the Xiri is packed away until the good weather returns.

When are you open?

Opening hours change with the season. We open from spring to autumn; to check today's hours, give us a call or look at our Instagram. In winter the beach bar is taken down.

Where exactly are you?

On Ses Oliveres beach, at Avinguda Víctor Rahola 25, in Cadaqués.

Do you serve paella?

Yes, rice dishes cooked in the paella pan. The most ordered is creamy rice with cuttlefish and red prawn. Minimum 2 people, about 20 minutes.

Is the menu in English?

Yes. The menu is in Catalan, Spanish, French and English, here on the website and as a PDF.
